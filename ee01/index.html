Before you order

How far in advance do I need to order?

We ask for at least one day's notice before your collection day. For large orders or custom items, two to three days is better. It gives us time to plan the bake.

How do I collect my order?

Come to the bakery at the time we agree. Your order will be bagged and labelled. If you are running late, let us know. We can usually hold orders for up to two hours.

Can I cancel or change my order?

Yes, but tell us as early as you can. If we have already started baking, we may not be able to cancel. Changes to quantity are usually fine with a day's notice.

Do you deliver?

Not yet. All orders are collected from the bakery. We are looking at delivery for the future, so follow us on Instagram to hear when that changes.

About The Corner Loaf

Marcus started The Corner Loaf after years of baking at home for friends and family. What began as a hobby became a small bakery when demand grew too big to keep up with.

Everything is made by hand in small batches. No mass production, no shortcuts.
